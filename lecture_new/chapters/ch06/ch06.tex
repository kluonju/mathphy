\begin{flushright}
\textit{“变则通，通则久。” ——《周易·系辞下》}
\footnote{From the ``Appended Statements'' of the 
\textit{Book of Changes}: ``Through change comes passage; 
through passage comes endurance.'' The quotation treats change 
as a way of exposing a path through a difficulty. 
In the calculus of variations, one studies small admissible 
changes of a trial function or path: a physical trajectory is 
distinguished by the fact that the relevant functional is 
stationary under every such variation.}
\end{flushright}

Calculus of variations studies extrema of functionals: quantities whose inputs are functions and whose outputs are numbers. It provides a common language for shortest paths, mechanics, constrained optimization, and field theories.
