\chapter{Mathematical Physics Equations}
\renewcommand{\currentchapterpath}{chapters/ch04/}

\begin{flushright}
\textit{“致知在格物。” ——《大学》}
\footnote{From \textit{The Great Learning}: 
``To extend knowledge, investigate things.'' Here, ``investigating 
things'' means attending carefully to phenomena and the principles that
 govern them. Mathematical physics follows this path by turning 
 observations, symmetries, and conservation laws into equations 
 that describe physical systems.}
\end{flushright}

Mathematical physics equations describe how physical quantities vary in space and time. This chapter derives the standard wave, diffusion, and potential equations, explains the data required to determine a physical solution, and develops separation of variables for representative boundary-value problems.
